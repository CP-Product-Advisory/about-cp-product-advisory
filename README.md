# CP Product Advisory

## Your business has grown. Why is it harder to run?

Reports disagree. Teams are working toward different priorities. Decisions that should be settled keep coming back to leadership.

CP Product Advisory helps leaders understand what is causing the problem, decide what needs to change, and make sure people know what they are responsible for.

## The businesses came together. Their ways of working didn't.

One team presents a number. Another disputes it. A project has three competing priorities, and nobody knows which commitment takes precedence.

Leadership spends another meeting settling something that was supposed to be settled last week.

The work starts by understanding what is happening before recommending another tool, changing the team, or asking people to take on more process.

The aim is practical:

- **Work from information you can trust.** Agree on what the numbers mean, where they come from, and who is responsible for them.
- **Give teams room to make decisions.** Establish which decisions belong with the team and which need leadership's judgment.
- **Give teams priorities they can act on.** Settle competing commitments and agree on responsibilities before work starts.

## Three ways to work together

### Strategic advisory

For leaders who know something needs to change but are not sure what to address first.

CP Product Advisory examines the situation, helps leadership work through the decisions, and recommends what to address next. Your team owns implementation unless a different scope is agreed.

### Interim product leadership

For businesses that need experienced product leadership for a defined period.

The engagement takes on agreed responsibilities across product priorities, roadmap decisions, requirements, and delivery coordination. Ownership, capacity, term, and handover are established before the work begins.

### Practical AI consulting

For businesses that want to understand where AI would help, why an existing effort is stuck, or how to make a specific workflow useful.

The work can start with one workflow or a broader organizational problem. Setup and support are available within an agreed scope. Human judgment stays with the people responsible for the business.

You do not need a larger advisory engagement to get practical AI help.

## What happens after you submit

You do not need to choose a service before starting.

1. **Complete the intake.** Describe the business challenge, what you have tried, and where progress is getting stuck.
2. **Have an initial conversation.** CP Product Advisory reviews your intake and emails you to arrange a 30-minute call. The conversation establishes whether there is a useful next step.
3. **Agree on the work.** If both sides decide to proceed, an offer is prepared around the situation discussed. Scope, responsibilities, and terms are agreed before paid work begins.

Submitting the intake does not commit you to a paid engagement. The initial conversation is separate from a paid review or advisory engagement.
